# BandwagonHost Datacenter Overview

BandwagonHost offers several route and datacenter options. The best choice depends on your users, budget, stock and product series.

## Common Directions

| Direction | Typical use |
|---|---|
| CN2 GIA-E Los Angeles | Balanced entry direction for many Chinese users |
| Hong Kong CN2 GIA | Asia-focused, latency-sensitive use |
| Tokyo / Osaka | Japan and Asia-focused alternatives |
| Singapore SG_8 | New Asia-focused option; verify stock and test target before relying on it |
| KVM PROMO | Budget-sensitive or lightweight use |
| ECOMMERCE SLA | Long-term or business-oriented projects |
| Dubai | Middle East route reference |

## How To Choose

Do not choose by ping alone. Compare:

- latency from your own network;
- product series;
- monthly transfer;
- bandwidth;
- stock status;
- billing cycle.
