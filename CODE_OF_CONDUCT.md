# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Please keep discussions respectful, factual and useful. This repository is about BandwagonHost route testing and datacenter research, not personal attacks or platform wars.

Examples of encouraged behavior:

- Share reproducible test results.
- Explain network conditions when reporting latency.
- Correct outdated information calmly.
- Keep buying discussions factual and related to the repository.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults or discriminatory language.
- Spam, unrelated promotions or repeated affiliate links.
- Posting private account information or credentials.
- Misleading claims about official status, guaranteed performance or guaranteed pricing.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content that violates these standards.
